EOQL: 27 commands

Nine operators, each in three grains. The bare name is Figure (the thing). A minus goes down to Ground (declarations, the schema underneath). A plus goes up to Pattern (what is built on top and written back). Prefix notation: the operator comes first and nests.

INS- kind:clip        declare a kind
INS  clip:7           make a thing
INS+ view:open        write a pattern back
EVA SEG- x            judge the things in a cut of the ground
NUL (CON a)           ask what a links to; change nothing
SEG 7 4               seven cut at four: 3 (subtraction)
SIG+ q:kind e         leave a trail: this kind of question was answered at e

A SIG is never part of an answer: remove every SIG and no determined answer changes.
Anything that stops early (a limit, a budget, the first hit) must say what range it left unread.
Evaporation is not a command: a trail's strength is read over a recent window.
